02 / How I work

Designer instincts. Builder hands.

Michael works across the concept and the underlying system: prototyping the feeling, structuring the rules, and shipping the playable thing.

A / 03

Design the loop

Translate a learning outcome, research question, or emotional beat into a clear interaction loop players can understand and remember.

B / 03

Build the system

Prototype in Unity, connect the state, dialogue, quest, adaptive, and input systems, then iterate until the design holds up in play.

C / 03

Respect the context

Make culturally resonant, accessible experiences that fit the people, platform, and real-world constraints around the game.
